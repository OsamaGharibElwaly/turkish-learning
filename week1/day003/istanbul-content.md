# 🇹🇷 Turkish Learning Guide: TÖMER A1 - Lesson 4 (Unit 1)

## 📚 Basic Vocabulary and Phrases (Temel Kelimeler ve İfadeler)

| Turkish (Türkçe) | English (İngilizce) | Usage Context (Kullanım Alanı) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Afiyet olsun!** | Enjoy your meal! / Bon appétit! | Said before or after eating. |
| **İyi dersler!** | Have a good lesson! | Said to students or teachers before a lesson starts. |
| **Bol şanslar!** | Good luck! | Said before exams or important events. |
| **Tebrikler!** | Congratulations! | Said for success, weddings, or achievements. |
| **Çok yaşa!** | Bless you! (lit. Live long!) | Said when someone sneezes (the responder says: "Sen de gör" - "And you too"). |
| **Lütfen!** | Please! | Used when politely asking for something. |
| **Özür dilerim!** | I'm sorry / I apologize. | For apologizing about a mistake or inconvenience. |
| **İyi geceler!** | Good night! | Said when saying goodbye before sleeping. |
| **Geçmiş olsun!** | Get well soon! / I hope it passes. | Said to someone who is sick or has gone through a difficult experience. |
| **Mutlu yıllar!** | Happy years! / Happy Birthday/New Year! | Said on birthdays or New Year's. |
| **Tamam!** | Okay / Alright! | For agreement or confirming understanding. |
| **İyi yolculuklar!** | Have a nice trip! | Said to travelers before they leave. |

## 🧩 Grammar and Language Structure (Dil Bilgisi ve Yapı)

### 1. Introducing Yourself and Nationality (Kendini Tanıtma ve Uyruk)

To express your name and nationality, we use the following structure:

> **Benim adım [name].** = My name is [name].
> **Ben [country]lıyım.** = I am from [country].
> **Ben [nationality]ım.** = I am [nationality].

#### Suffix Rule (Ek):

The suffix **"-lı / -li / -lu / -lü"** is used to indicate belonging to a country, and it is followed by the first-person suffix **"-yım / -yim / -yum / -yüm"** (with the buffer consonant "y" to prevent vowel collision).

| Country (Ülke) | Nationality / Origin (Uyruk) | Full Example (Örnek Cümle) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Almanya (Germany) | Almanyalıyım / Almanım | Ben Almanyalıyım ve Almanım. (I am from Germany and I am German.) |
| Avusturya (Austria) | Avusturyalıyım | Ben Avusturyalıyım. (I am from Austria.) |
| Türkiye (Turkey) | Türkiyeliyim / Türküm | Ben Türkiyeliyim ve Türküm. (I am from Turkey and I am Turkish.) |
| Fransa (France) | Fransalıyım / Fransızım | Ben Fransalıyım ve Fransızım. (I am from France and I am French.) |
| Çin (China) | Çinliyim | Ben Çinliyim. (I am from China.) |
| Nijerya (Nigeria) | Nijeryalıyım | Ben Nijeryalıyım. (I am from Nigeria.) |

## 💬 Sample Dialogue for Practice (Diyalog Örneği)

*Use the following phrases to write a dialogue with your classmate:*
- Senin adın ne? (What's your name?)
- Nasılsın? (How are you?)
- Nerelisin? (Where are you from?)
- Memnun oldum. (Nice to meet you.)

### 📝 Sample Dialogue (Örnek Diyalog):

> **Ahmet:** Merhaba! Senin adın ne?  
> *(Hello! What's your name?)*
>
> **Bai:** Merhaba! Benim adım Bai. Senin adın ne?  
> *(Hello! My name is Bai. What's your name?)*
>
> **Ahmet:** Benim adım Ahmet. Nasılsın?  
> *(My name is Ahmet. How are you?)*
>
> **Bai:** İyiyim, teşekkür ederim. Sen nasılsın?  
> *(I'm fine, thank you. How are you?)*
>
> **Ahmet:** Ben de iyiyim, sağ ol. Nerelisin?  
> *(I'm fine too, thanks. Where are you from?)*
>
> **Bai:** Ben Çinliyim. Sen nerelisin?  
> *(I am from China. Where are you from?)*
>
> **Ahmet:** Ben Türkiyeliyim ve Türküm. Memnun oldum!  
> *(I am from Turkey and I am Turkish. Nice to meet you!)*
>
> **Bai:** Ben de memnun oldum! İyi dersler!  
> *(Nice to meet you too! Have a good lesson!)*
>
> **Ahmet:** Teşekkürler, sana da iyi dersler!  
> *(Thanks, you too!)*

## 💡 Study and Memorization Tips (Çalışma İpuçları)

1. **Flashcards:** Create double-sided flashcards (Turkish ↔ English) for the 12 basic phrases and review them daily.

2. **Vowel Harmony Rule (Ünlü Uyumu):** Practice pronouncing the suffixes (`-lı, -li, -lu, -lü`) with different country names out loud to get used to vowel harmony.

3. **Role Playing (Rol Yapma):** Practice the sample dialogue with a study partner or in front of a mirror. Change the name and nationality each time (e.g., pretend you are "Fransalıyım" then "Nijeryalıyım").

4. **Daily Usage:** Try to use at least one phrase from the list in your daily life (e.g., `Afiyet olsun` when eating, or `Geçmiş olsun` when hearing about a friend's illness).

## 🔍 Additional Learning Resources

### Vowel Harmony Reference (Ünlü Uyumu)

Turkish vowels follow a harmony system. The suffix changes based on the last vowel in the word:

| Last Vowel | Suffix Used | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| a, ı | -lı | Çin + li → Çinli |
| e, i | -li | Alman + ya + lı → Almanyalı |
| o, u | -lu | (example: Avusturya + lı → Avusturyalı) |
| ö, ü | -lü | (example: Türk + üm → Türküm) |
