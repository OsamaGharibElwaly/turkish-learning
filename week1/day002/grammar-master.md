Here is your **A1 & A2 Turkish Grammar Master Guide**. To keep this beginner-friendly, practical, and concise, I have grouped all core rules into logical categories. Each rule includes its meaning, formula, and a real-life Turkish/English example.

---

### 🟢 MODULE 1: The Foundations (A1)

**1. Vowel Harmony & Buffer 'Y'**
*   **Meaning:** Suffixes change vowels to match the last vowel of the root word. A buffer **'y'** prevents two vowels from crashing.
*   **Formula:** Back (a, ı, o, u) → **-a/-ı/-u** | Front (e, i, ö, ü) → **-e/-i/-ü**
*   **Example:** *Araba* + *y* + *ım* = **Arabam** (My car). *Ev* + *im* = **Evim** (My house).

**2. Consonant Hardening (Fıstıkçı Şahap)**
*   **Meaning:** If a word ends in **F, S, T, K, Ç, Ş, H, P**, a suffix starting with *d, c, g* hardens to *t, ç, k*.
*   **Formula:** Word (F,S,T,K,Ç,Ş,H,P) + **-ta/-te** (instead of -da/-de)
*   **Example:** *Kitap* + *da* = **Kitapta** (At the book). *Ders* + *de* = **Derste** (In the lesson).

**3. Plural Suffix (-lar / -ler)**
*   **Meaning:** Makes a noun plural ("s" in English).
*   **Formula:** Noun + **-lar** (back vowels) / **-ler** (front vowels)
*   **Example:** **Okullar** (Schools), **Evler** (Houses).

### 🟡 MODULE 2: Noun Cases (A1)
*Answers: Where? To where? From where? What?*

**4. Locative Case (-da / -de / -ta / -te)**
*   **Meaning:** Static location: "in, at, on".
*   **Formula:** Noun + **-da/-de** (hardens to **-ta/-te** if ending in Fıstıkçı Şahap).
*   **Example:** **Evdeyim.** (I am at home.) **Sınıfta.** (In the classroom.)

**5. Dative Case (-a / -e / -ya / -ye)**
*   **Meaning:** Motion or direction: "to, towards".
*   **Formula:** Noun + **-a/-e** (add buffer **'y'** if noun ends in a vowel).
*   **Example:** **Okula gidiyorum.** (I am going to school.) **İstanbul'a** (To Istanbul).

**6. Ablative Case (-dan / -den / -tan / -ten)**
*   **Meaning:** Separation or origin: "from, out of, because of".
*   **Formula:** Noun + **-dan/-den** (hardens to **-tan/-ten** if ending in Fıstıkçı Şahap).
*   **Example:** **İşten geldim.** (I came from work.) **Soğuktan** (Because of the cold).

**7. Accusative Case (-ı / -i / -u / -ü / -yı / -yi / -yu / -yü)**
*   **Meaning:** Definite direct object: "the [noun]". (Answers *Neyi?* / *Kimi?*)
*   **Formula:** Noun + **-ı/-i/-u/-ü** (add buffer **'y'** if noun ends in a vowel).
*   **Example:** **Elmayı yedim.** (I ate *the* apple.) **Seni seviyorum.** (I love *you*.)

---

### 🟠 MODULE 3: Possession & Compounds (A1/A2)

**8. Possessive Suffixes (Benim, Senin, Onun...)**
*   **Meaning:** Shows ownership: "my, your, his/her, our, your, their".
*   **Formula:** Noun + Possessive Suffix (*-m, -n, -sı, -mız, -nız, -ları*)
*   **Example:** **Arabam** (My car), **Evin** (Your house), **Okulumuz** (Our school).

**9. Indefinite Compound Nouns (Belirtisiz İsim Tamlaması)**
*   **Meaning:** Creates compound nouns describing a type/category ("coffee cup", "school bag").
*   **Formula:** [Noun 1: bare] + [Noun 2 + 3rd person possessive suffix]
*   **Example:** **Diş fırçası** (Toothbrush), **Okul çantası** (School bag).

---

### 🔴 MODULE 4: Core Tenses (A1/A2)

**10. Present Continuous (-iyor)**
*   **Meaning:** Actions happening right now.
*   **Formula:** Verb Stem + **-iyor** + Personal Suffix
*   **Example:** **Geliyorum.** (I am coming.) **Ne yapıyorsun?** (What are you doing?)

**11. Simple Past (-dı / -di)**
*   **Meaning:** Completed actions in the past (I saw, I did).
*   **Formula:** Verb Stem + **-dı/-di/-du/-dü** + Personal Suffix
*   **Example:** **Dün sinemaya gittim.** (I went to the cinema yesterday.)

**12. Future Tense (-acak / -ecek)**
*   **Meaning:** Actions that will happen; plans or promises.
*   **Formula:** Verb Stem + **-acak/-ecek** (softens to *-acağ/-eceğ* for "I" and "We") + Personal Suffix.
*   **Example:** **Yarın geleceğim.** (I will come tomorrow.) **Yapacağız.** (We will do.)

**13. Aorist / Simple Present (-r / -ar / -er)**
*   **Meaning:** General truths, habits, routines, or polite requests.
*   **Formula:** Verb Stem + **-r / -ar / -er** + Personal Suffix
*   **Example:** **Her gün kahve içerim.** (I drink coffee every day.) **Yapar mısın?** (Could you do it? - *Polite*)

---

### 🟣 MODULE 5: Moods & Modals (A1/A2)

**14. Imperative Mood (Emir Kipi)**
*   **Meaning:** Commands, orders, or polite requests.
*   **Formula:** 
  *   *Sen* (informal): Bare Stem (**Gel!** = Come!)
  *   *Siz* (formal/plural): Stem + **-ın/-in/-un/-ün** (**Gelin!** = Please come!)
*   **Example:** **Lütfen oturun.** (Please sit down.) **Yapma!** (Don't do it!)

**15. Ability / Potential (-abil / -ebil)**
*   **Meaning:** "Can, could, be able to, might".
*   **Formula:** Verb Stem + **-abil/-ebil** + Tense + Personal Suffix. *(No consonant softening!)*
*   **Example:** **Türkçe konuşabiliyorum.** (I can speak Turkish.) **Yarın yağabilir.** (It might rain tomorrow.)

**16. Necessity (-malı / -meli OR lazım / gerek)**
*   **Meaning:** "Must, have to, need to".
*   **Formula:** 
  *   Verb + **-malı/-meli** + Personal Suffix (*Gitmeliyim* = I must go).
  *   Verb + **-mak/-mek** + Possessive + **lazım** (*Gitmem lazım* = I need to go).
*   **Example:** **Erken kalkman lazım.** (You need to wake up early.)

---

### 🔵 MODULE 6: Describing Things (A2)

**17. Adjective-Making Suffixes (-lı, -sız, -sal)**
*   **Meaning:** Turns nouns into adjectives.
*   **Formula:** Noun + **-lı/-li** (having/with) OR **-sız/-siz** (without)
*   **Example:** **Tuzlu** (Salty), **Şekersiz** (Sugar-free), **Kültürel** (Cultural).

**18. Intensifying Adjectives (Pekiştirme)**
*   **Meaning:** Emphasizes an adjective ("brand new", "snow white").
*   **Formula:** 1st Syllable + **p/m/r/s** + Full Adjective (Written as ONE word).
*   **Example:** **Yepyeni** (Brand new), **Bembeyaz** (Snow white), **Tertemiz** (Spotless).

**19. Comparatives & Superlatives**
*   **Meaning:** Comparing things ("more than", "the most").
*   **Formula:** 
  *   Comparative: Noun A, Noun B + **-dan/-den** + **daha** + Adjective.
  *   Superlative: **en** + Adjective.
*   **Example:** **Ali, Veli'den daha uzun.** (Ali is taller than Veli.) **En iyi** (The best).

---

### 🟤 MODULE 7: Connecting Ideas (A2)

**20. Conjunctions (Bağlaçlar)**
*   **Meaning:** Links clauses together.
*   **Key Words:** **ve** (and), **ama** (but), **çünkü** (because), **veya** (or), **ki** (that / so that).
*   **Example:** **Geldim ama o yoktu.** (I came but he wasn't there.) **Biliyorum ki yorgunsun.** (I know that you are tired.)

**21. Adverbial Clauses / Gerunds (Zarf-Fiiller)**
*   **Meaning:** Links two actions by time or method ("by doing", "before doing", "after doing").
*   **Formulas & Examples:**
  *   *-erek/-arak* (by doing): **Koşarak geldi.** (He came running.)
  *   *-ıp/-ip* (and then): **Gidip gördüm.** (I went and saw.)
  *   *-madan önce* (before): **Yemeden önce** (Before eating).
  *   *-dık(t)an sonra* (after): **Yedikten sonra** (After eating).
