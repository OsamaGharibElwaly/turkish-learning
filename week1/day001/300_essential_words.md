## 🚀 How to Use This List Effectively

1. **The "5-a-Day" Rule**: Don't try to memorize all 300 at once. Pick 5 words per day. In 2 months, you will have mastered this entire list.
2. **Combine with Patterns**: Take a word from this list and plug it into the **30 High-Frequency Sentence Patterns** you learned earlier. 
   - *Example*: Word #171 (Su) + Pattern #9 (___ lazım) = **Su lazım.** (I need water).
3. **Use Spaced Repetition**: Copy this Markdown table directly into **Anki**, **Quizlet**, or **Notion**. Put the Turkish word on the front and the English + pronunciation on the back.
4. **Learn the Plurals & Cases**: Once you know the root word (e.g., *ev*), practice saying it with suffixes: *evler* (houses), *evde* (at home), *eve* (to home).
5. **Focus on the Top 100 First**: Words 1–100 make up roughly 50% of daily spoken Turkish. Master those before moving to the specialized vocabulary (like food or objects).
