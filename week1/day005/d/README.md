## 💬 2. Grammar: Asking "Where are you?" (Neredesin?)

### The Pattern:
**Nerede?** (Where?) + **-de/-da/-te/-ta** (locative suffix)

### Personal Endings for "to be" (olmak):

| Turkish | English |
|---------|---------|
| **Evdeyim** | I am at home |
| **Evdesin** | You are at home |
| **Evde** | He/She/It is at home |
| **Evdeyiz** | We are at home |
| **Evdesiniz** | You (plural/formal) are at home |
| **Evde(ler)** | They are at home |

## 📚 3. Example Dialogues from the Page

**Dialogue 1: Asking where someone is**
- A: *Neredesin?* (Where are you?)
- B: *Evdeyim.* (I'm at home.)

**Dialogue 2: Stating your identity**
- *Ben öğrenciyim.* (I am a student.)

**Dialogue 3: Asking if someone is at a location**
- A: *Sen işte misin?* (Are you at work?)
- B: *Evet, işteyim.* (Yes, I'm at work.)
- B: *Hayır, işte değilim.* (No, I'm not at work.)

## 🔑 4. Key Grammar Points

### A. Locative Case (-de/-da/-te/-ta)
Add this suffix to indicate **location** (in, on, at):

- **ev** (house) → **evde** (at home)
- **okul** (school) → **okulda** (at school)
- **iş** (work) → **işte** (at work) *('ş' is a hard consonant)*
- **park** (park) → **parkta** (in the park) *('k' is a hard consonant)*
- **eczane** (pharmacy) → **eczanede** (at the pharmacy)

### B. Negative Form: değil (not)
To say you are **NOT** at a location:
- *İşte **değilim**.* (I am **not** at work.)
- *Evde **değilsin**.* (You are **not** at home.)
- *Okulda **değil**.* (He/She is **not** at school.)

### C. Question Form with "mi"
- *Evde **misin**?* (Are you at home?)
- *İşte **mi**?* (Is he/she at work?)
- *Okulda **mıyız**?* (Are we at school?)

Example answers:
- *Semtimde eczane var.* (There is a pharmacy in my neighborhood.)
- *Semtimde hastane yok.* (There is no hospital in my neighborhood.)

### Exercise 3: Who is in these places?
**Yukarıdaki yerlerde kimler var?**

Example answers:
- *Hastane**de** doktorlar var.* (There are doctors in the hospital.)
- *Okul**da** öğrenciler var.* (There are students at school.)
- *Market**te** müşteriler var.* (There are customers in the market.)
- *Fırın**da** fırıncı var.* (There is a baker in the bakery.)

## 🗣️ 6. Practice Sentences

**Asking where:**
- *Neredesin?* → *Kafe**deyim**.* (Where are you? → I'm at the cafe.)
- *Ali nerede?* → *Ali park**ta**.* (Where is Ali? → Ali is in the park.)
- *Doktor nerede?* → *Doktor hastane**de**.* (Where is the doctor? → The doctor is at the hospital.)

**Yes/No questions:**
- *Sen okulda mısın?* → *Evet, okuldayım.* (Are you at school? → Yes, I'm at school.)
- *O işte mi?* → *Hayır, işte değil. Evde.* (Is he at work? → No, he's not at work. He's at home.)

**Negatives:**
- *Ben evde değilim.* (I'm not at home.)
- *O okulda değil.* (He/She is not at school.)

## ✅ Quick Tips

1. **Hard consonant rule:** If a word ends in **ç, f, h, k, p, s, ş, t**, use **-ta/-te**
   - *park → parkta*
   - *iş → işte*
   - *ağaç → ağaçta*

2. **Vowel harmony:** Match the vowel in the suffix to the last vowel in the word
   - *ev**de*** (e → e)
   - *okul**da*** (u → a)

3. **"Değil" placement:** Comes **after** the location
   - *Evde **değilim*** (NOT *Değilim evde*)
