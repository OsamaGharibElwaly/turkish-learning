# 🇹🇷 Turkish Grammar: The Locative Case (-DA / -DE / -TA / -TE)

This topic covers the **Locative Case** (Bulunma Durumu). In English, this corresponds to the prepositions **"in," "on," "at,"** or **"with."** In Arabic, it corresponds to **"في" (in/at), "على" (on), or "مع" (with)**.

It is used to answer two main questions:
1. **Nerede?** (Where?)
2. **Kimde?** (Who has it? / With whom?)

---

### 1. The 4 Forms of the Suffix (Vowel Harmony)
Just like the question particle *mI*, the locative suffix changes based on the last vowel of the word.

| Last Vowel of the Word | Suffix (Normal) | Suffix (Hard Consonant*) | Example | Translation |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **a, ı** | **-da** | **-ta** | *Okul**da*** / *Park**ta*** | At school / In the park |
| **e, i** | **-de** | **-te** | *Ev**de*** / *Ofis**te*** | At home / In the office |
| **o, u** | **-da** | **-ta** | *Araba**da*** / *Çanta**da*** | In the car / In the bag |
| **ö, ü** | **-de** | **-te** | *Göz**de*** / *Üst**te*** | In the eye / On top |

---

### 2. The "Hard Consonant" Rule (Fıstıkçı Şahap)
*This is a very important rule in Turkish!*

If a word ends in one of these **8 hard consonants**: **Ç, F, H, K, P, S, Ş, T**, the suffix changes from **d** to **t**.
*(Memory trick for Turkish students: **Fıstıkçı Şahap**)*

*   **Park** ends in **k** ➡️ Park**ta** (NOT Parkda)
*   **Ofis** ends in **s** ➡️ Ofis**te** (NOT Ofisde)
*   **Ağaç** ends in **ç** ➡️ Ağaç**ta** (NOT Ağaçda)
*   **Masa** ends in **a** (soft) ➡️ Masa**da**

---

### 3. Usage 1: Nerede? (Where?)
Used to state the **location** of a person or object.

*   **Öğrenciler nerede?** (Where are the students?)
    *   *Öğrenciler sınıf**ta**.* (The students are **in** the classroom.)
*   **Bilgisayar nerede?** (Where is the computer?)
    *   *Bilgisayar masa**da**.* (The computer is **on** the table.)
*   **Taksim nerede?** (Where is Taksim?)
    *   *Taksim İstanbul'**da**.* (Taksim is **in** Istanbul.)

---

### 4. Usage 2: Kimde? (Who has it?)
In Turkish, to say someone "has" something temporarily, you say the object is **"on/with"** that person.

*   **Kalem kimde?** (Who has the pen?)
    *   *Kalem öğretmen**de**.* (The teacher has the pen / The pen is **with** the teacher.)
*   **Çanta kimde?** (Who has the bag?)
    *   *Çanta Ahmet'**te**.* (Ahmet has the bag / The bag is **with** Ahmet.)
*   **Telefon kimde?** (Who has the phone?)
    *   *Telefon Merve'**de**.* (Merve has the phone.)

### 💡 Pro-Tip for Proper Names (Cities, People)
When adding suffixes to proper names (like *Ahmet, Hasan, İstanbul*), you must use an **apostrophe (')** to separate the name from the suffix.
*   *İstanbul* ➡️ İstanbul'**da**
*   *Ahmet* ➡️ Ahmet'**te**
*   *Merve* ➡️ Merve'**de**

This page focuses on the existence of things and people using **Var** (There is/are) and **Yok** (There isn't/are not). It also covers how to ask "What is there?" and "Who is there?".

Here is the breakdown of the grammar and the solutions to the exercises.

### 📚 Grammar: Var & Yok

*   **Var:** Means "There is" or "There are". It is also used to show possession (like "I have").
    *   *Evde bilgisayar **var**.* (There is a computer in the house / The house has a computer.)
    *   *Bende kalem **var**.* (I have a pen / Literally: On me there is a pen.)
*   **Yok:** Means "There isn't" or "There aren't".
    *   *Sınıfta öğretmen **yok**.* (There is no teacher in the class.)

###  Asking Questions

1.  **Var mı? / Yok mu?** (Is there? / Are there?)
    *   *Sokakta araba **var mı**?* (Is there a car on the street?)
    *   *Cevap:* Evet, var. / Hayır, yok.

2.  **Ne var? / Neler var?** (What is there? / What things are there?)
    *   Use **Ne var?** for a general answer or singular.
    *   Use **Neler var?** when expecting a list of plural items.
    *   *Odada **ne var**?* -> *Odada yatak var.* (There is a bed in the room.)
    *   *Okulda **neler var**?* -> *Okulda sınıflar, bahçe, kantin var.* (There are classrooms, a garden, a canteen at school.)

3.  **Kim var? / Kimler var?** (Who is there? / Who are there?)
    *   *Hastanede **kim var**?* -> *Doktor var.*
    *   *Sınıfta **kimler var**?* -> *Öğrenciler var.*

### ✅ Solutions to Exercises

#### **Questions 4-9 (Top Left Column)**
*These require general knowledge or personal answers.*

**4. Televizyonda ne var?** (What is on TV?)
*   *Televizyonda haberler var.* (There is news on TV.) OR *Film var.*

**5. Sokakta kimler var?** (Who are on the street?)
*   *Sokakta insanlar var.* (There are people on the street.)

**6. Sınıfta öğretmen var mı?** (Is there a teacher in the class?)
*   *Evet, var.* (Yes, there is.)

**7. Okulda hafta sonu ders var mı?** (Are there classes at school on the weekend?)
*   *Hayır, yok.* (No, there aren't.)

**8. İstanbul’da deniz var mı?** (Is there a sea in Istanbul?)
*   *Evet, var.* (Yes, there is.)

**9. Sende sözlük var mı?** (Do you have a dictionary?)
