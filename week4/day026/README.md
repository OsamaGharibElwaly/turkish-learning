### Key grammar: **-miş**

The story uses **Belirsiz Geçmiş Zaman**.

**-miş / -mış / -muş / -müş**

It can express:

* something you heard from someone
* something you discovered later
* a story or legend
* something whose direct evidence you don't have

### Examples

**Bir padişah varmış.**
→ There was supposedly a sultan.

**Çok güzel bir kızı varmış.**
→ He apparently had a very beautiful daughter.

**Kız çok mutluymuş.**
→ Apparently, the girl was very happy.

# 3. 🧠 BELİRSİZ GEÇMİŞ ZAMAN

## Reported / Inferential Past

### Verb

**gelmek → gelmiş**
to come → apparently came / has come

**gitmek → gitmiş**
to go → apparently went

**yapmak → yapmış**
to do → apparently did

**olmak → olmuş**
to become/be → apparently became

### Negative

**gelmemiş**
→ apparently didn't come

**gitmemiş**
→ apparently didn't go

### Noun sentences

**O öğretmenmiş.**
→ Apparently, he/she is/was a teacher.

**Babası emekliymiş.**
→ Apparently, his/her father was retired.

# 5. 🔗 BAĞLAÇLAR — Conjunctions

## A. Hem … hem (de)

### Both … and

Used when **both things are true**.

> **Hem müzik dinliyor hem de çalışıyor.**
> He is both listening to music and working.

> **Hem Antalya’ya hem de İzmir’e gittim.**
> I went to both Antalya and İzmir.

### Structure

**hem + A + hem de + B**

---

## B. Ne … ne (de)

### Neither … nor

Used when **both things are negative**.

> **Ne kahve ne de çay içiyorum.**
> I drink neither coffee nor tea.

> **Ne paramız ne de zamanımız var.**
> We have neither money nor time.

⚠️ Important:

Turkish normally keeps the **main verb grammatically positive**.

❌ Ne kahve ne çay içmiyorum.
✅ **Ne kahve ne de çay içiyorum.**

Meaning:

> I don't drink either coffee or tea.

---

## C. Ya … ya (da)

### Either … or

Used for **choice / alternatives**.

> **Ya çay ya da kahve içeceğim.**
> I will drink either tea or coffee.

> **Ya metrobüsle ya da vapurla gel.**
> Come either by metrobus or by ferry.

### Structure

**ya + A + ya da + B**

# 6. 🗣️ DİYE — “saying / thinking / asking”

**diye** connects direct speech with the reporting verb.

### Asking

> **Mert bana “Nereye gidiyorsun?” diye sordu.**

→ Mert asked me, **“Where are you going?”**

### Answering

> **Ben ona “Eve gidiyorum.” diye cevap verdim.**

→ I answered him, **“I am going home.”**

### Thinking

> **“Bu doğru.” diye düşündüm.**

→ I thought, **“This is correct.”**

### Common verbs

| Turkish           | English            |
| ----------------- | ------------------ |
| diye sormak       | ask, saying...     |
| diye cevap vermek | answer, saying...  |
| diye düşünmek     | think, thinking... |
| diye söylemek     | say, saying...     |
