# 🇹🇷 Turkish Grammar: VAR, YOK, NEREDE, KİMDE

This topic covers **existence**, **location**, and **possession** in Turkish.

---

## 1. VAR and YOK (Existence)

### **VAR** = There is/are, exists, have
### **YOK** = There isn't/aren't, doesn't exist, don't have

Unlike English, Turkish doesn't use "have" for possession. Instead, it uses **var/yok** with locative cases.

### Examples:

| Turkish | English |
|---------|---------|
| **Sınıfta öğrenciler var.** | There are students in the class. |
| **Masada kitap var mı?** | Is there a book on the table? |
| **Hayır, masada kitap yok.** | No, there isn't a book on the table. |
| **Okulda kantin var.** | There is a canteen at school. |
| **Sınıflarda öğrenciler yok.** | There are no students in the classrooms. |

---

## 2. NEREDE? (Where?)

**Nerede?** asks about the **location** of people or things.

### Examples:

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Öğretmen nerede?** | **Öğretmen ofiste.** (The teacher is in the office.) |
| **Öğrenciler nerede?** | **Öğrenciler kantinde.** (The students are in the canteen.) |
| **Kitaplar nerede?** | **Kitaplar masada.** (The books are on the table.) |

> 💡 **Grammar Note:** To answer "nerede?", use the **locative case (-de/-da/-te/-ta)**
> - okul**da** (at school)
> - sınıf**ta** (in the classroom)
> - ofis**te** (in the office)
> - masa**da** (on the table)

---

## 3. KİMDE? (With whom? / Who has?)

**Kimde?** asks **who has something** or **with whom something is located**.

### Examples:

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **Kalem kimde?** | **Kalem Ahmet'te.** (Ahmet has the pen / The pen is with Ahmet.) |
| **Kitaplar kimde?** | **Kitaplar öğretmenlerde.** (The teachers have the books.) |
| **Anahtar kimde?** | **Anahtar bende.** (I have the key / The key is with me.) |

> 💡 **Grammar Note:** To answer "kimde?", add **-de/-da** to the person's name:
> - Ahmet**te** (with Ahmet)
> - Ayşe**de** (with Ayşe)
> - ben**de** (with me)
> - sen**de** (with you)

##  Reading Text: OKUL (School) - Translation

**Merhaba! Benim adım Kate. Ben İspanyalıyım. Burası benim okulum. Okul Tophane'de. Okul 6 katlı. Alt katta ofisler var. Ofisler büyük. Ofislerde masalar, koltuklar, bilgisayarlar, dolaplar var. Sekreterler ofiste.**

> Hello! My name is Kate. I am Spanish. This is my school. The school is in Tophane. The school has 6 floors. On the ground floor, there are offices. The offices are big. In the offices, there are tables, chairs, computers, and cabinets. The secretaries are in the office.

**2., 3., 4. ve 5. katlarda sınıflar var. Okulda 10 sınıf var. Her sınıfta 15 öğrenci var. Sınıflarda; sandalyeler, sıralar, kalemler, kitaplar, sözlükler, defterler, perdeler, lambalar, bilgisayar ve yazı tahtası var. Masalarda kitaplar, kalemler, defterler ve silgiler var. Şimdi sınıflarda öğrenciler yok, çünkü öğrenciler teneffüste.**

> On the 2nd, 3rd, 4th, and 5th floors, there are classrooms. There are 10 classrooms in the school. There are 15 students in each classroom. In the classrooms, there are chairs, desks, pens, books, dictionaries, notebooks, curtains, lamps, computers, and whiteboards. On the tables, there are books, pens, notebooks, and erasers. Right now, there are no students in the classrooms because the students are on break.

**Okulda çok güzel bir kantin var. Kantin üst katta. Kantinde; sandalyeler, masalar, televizyon, yiyecekler ve içecekler var. Kantin çok kalabalık. Bütün öğrenciler kantinde.**

> There is a very nice canteen at the school. The canteen is on the top floor. In the canteen, there are chairs, tables, television, food, and drinks. The canteen is very crowded. All the students are in the canteen.

## 🎯 Key Grammar Points

### 1. **Locative Case (-de/-da/-te/-ta)**
Used to indicate location:
- okul**da** (at school)
- sınıf**ta** (in class)
- ev**de** (at home)
- masa**da** (on the table)

### 2. **Var/Yok Sentence Structure**
**Location + var/yok**
- **Sınıfta öğrenciler var.** (There are students in the class.)
- **Masada kitap yok.** (There is no book on the table.)

### 3. **Asking "Is there...?"**
**Location + noun + var mı?**
- **Masada kitap var mı?** (Is there a book on the table?)
- **Sınıfta öğrenci var mı?** (Are there students in the class?)

### 4. **Kimde (With whom?)**
- **Kalem kimde?** → **Ahmet'te.** (Who has the pen? → Ahmet has it.)
- **Kitaplar kimde?** → **Öğretmende.** (Who has the books? → The teacher has them.)

## 📝 Practice Sentences

**VAR:**
- Evde bir kedi var. (There is a cat at home.)
- Çantamda kitaplar var. (There are books in my bag.)
- Okulda kantin var. (There is a canteen at school.)

**YOK:**
- Sınıfta öğretmen yok. (There is no teacher in the class.)
- Masada kalem yok. (There is no pen on the table.)
- Evde kimse yok. (There is no one at home.)

**NEREDE?**
- Araba nerede? → Garajda. (Where is the car? → In the garage.)
- Ahmet nerede? → Okulda. (Where is Ahmet? → At school.)

**KİMDE?**
- Telefon kimde? → Bende. (Who has the phone? → I have it.)
- Anahtarlar kimde? → Annemde. (Who has the keys? → My mother has them.)

### 📝 Grammar Structure: Var & Yok (There is / There isn't)

You use **Var** to say something exists in a place, and **Yok** to say it doesn't. You usually combine this with the location suffix **-da/-de/-ta/-te** (in/on/at).

*   **Structure:** [Location] + [Object] + **var/yok**.
*   **Example:** *Masada bilgisayar var.* (There is a computer on the table.)
*   **Example:** *Odada yatak yok.* (There is no bed in the room.)

### ✍️ Exercise 12: YAZMA (Writing)
**Task:** Look at the pictures. What is in the rooms? Write the differences.
The example given is: *"Resim 4'te pembe sandalye var. Resim 1'de pembe sandalye yok. Kahverengi sandalye var..."* (In picture 4 there is a pink chair. In picture 1 there is no pink chair. There is a brown chair...)

**Here are more examples you can write to complete the exercise:**

1.  **Comparing Beds:**
    *   *Resim 4'te kırmızı yatak var.* (There is a red bed in picture 4.)
    *   *Resim 2'de mavi yatak var.* (There is a blue bed in picture 2.)

2.  **Comparing Computers:**
    *   *Resim 2'de bilgisayar var.* (There is a computer in picture 2.)
    *   *Resim 1'de bilgisayar yok.* (There is no computer in picture 1.)

3.  **Comparing Chairs:**
    *   *Resim 3'te pembe sandalye var.* (There is a pink chair in picture 3.)
    *   *Resim 1'de kahverengi sandalye var.* (There is a brown chair in picture 1.)

4.  **Comparing Rugs (Halı):**
    *   *Resim 3'te kırmızı halı var.* (There is a red rug in picture 3.)
    *   *Resim 2'de halı yok.* (There is no rug in picture 2.)
