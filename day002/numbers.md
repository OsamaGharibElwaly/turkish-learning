Welcome to **Day 2** of your Turkish learning journey! Today, we are tackling **Numbers (Sayılar)**. 

Instead of memorizing a massive list of every number from 1 to 1,000,000,000,000, Turkish numbers are actually very logical. Once you learn the **base blocks** and the **rules**, you can build *any* number up to a trillion (and beyond!) easily.

Here is your complete guide to the rules of Turkish numbers, followed by practice exercises.

---

### 🌟 The 3 Golden Rules of Turkish Numbers
Before we look at the words, you must know these three rules. They make Turkish numbers much easier than English numbers!

1. **No "AND":** In English, we say "One hundred **and** five". In Turkish, there is no "and". You just say the words: *yüz beş* (literally: hundred five).
2. **No Plurals:** Numbers never take an "-s" or "-lar" suffix. It is always *iki bin* (two thousand), never *iki binler*.
3. **Read Left to Right:** Just like in English, you start with the biggest number and go down. (e.g., 2,345 = *iki bin üç yüz kırk beş*).

---

### 🧱 Step 1: The Base Blocks (0 - 10)
Memorize these first. They are the foundation of everything.

*   **0** = sıfır
*   **1** = bir
*   **2** = iki
*   **3** = üç
*   **4** = dört
*   **5** = beş
*   **6** = altı
*   **7** = yedi
*   **8** = sekiz
*   **9** = dokuz
*   **10** = on

*(To make 11-19, just put **on** in front: 11 = on bir, 15 = on beş, 19 = on dokuz).*

---

### 🧱 Step 2: The Tens (20 - 90)
Notice that 30, 40, 50, and 70 have slight spelling changes from their base numbers. Memorize these 8 words:

*   **20** = yirmi
*   **30** = o**t**uz *(not "üç" + "on")*
*   **40** = **kırk** *(not "dört" + "on")*
*   **50** = e**ll**i *(not "beş" + "on")*
*   **60** = altmış
*   **70** = **yet**miş *(not "yedi" + "on")*
*   **80** = seksen
*   **90** = doksan

*(To make numbers like 24, just combine them with a space: 20 + 4 = **yirmi dört**).*

---

### 🧱 Step 3: The Hundreds (100 - 900)
The word for 100 is **yüz**. To make the other hundreds, just put the base number (1-9) in front of it.

*   **100** = yüz *(See Rule below!)*
*   **200** = iki yüz
*   **300** = üç yüz
*   **400** = dört yüz
*   **500** = beş yüz
*   **600** = altı yüz
*   **700** = yedi yüz
*   **800** = sekiz yüz
*   **900** = dokuz yüz

> ⚠️ **CRITICAL EXCEPTION FOR 100:** 
> In English, we say "**one** hundred". In Turkish, we **DO NOT** say "bir yüz". We just say **yüz**. 
> *However*, if it is part of a larger number, you don't say "bir" either. 
> *   100 = **yüz**
> *   115 = **yüz on beş** (NOT bir yüz...)
> *   1,100 = **bin yüz** (NOT bir bin bir yüz...)

---

### 🏢 Step 4: The "Big Four" (1,000 to 1 Trillion)
These are your multipliers. Just like 100, the word for 1,000 (**bin**) drops the "bir" when it stands alone. But for Million, Billion, and Trillion, you **must** use "bir".

*   **1,000** = bin *(NOT bir bin)*
*   **1,000,000** = **bir** milyon
*   **1,000,000,000** = **bir** milyar *(Note: 1 Billion in US English = 1 Milyar in Turkish)*
*   **1,000,000,000,000** = **bir** trilyon

> ⚠️ **CRITICAL EXCEPTION FOR 1,000:**
> Just like 100, we do not say "bir bin". We just say **bin**. 
> *   1,000 = **bin**
> *   2,000 = **iki bin**
> *   1,500 = **bin beş yüz** (NOT bir bin beş yüz)

---

### 🏗️ How to Build ANY Number (The Formula)
You just stack the blocks from left to right, separating each word with a **space**.

**Example 1: 45**
*   40 (kırk) + 5 (beş) = **kırk beş**

**Example 2: 328**
*   300 (üç yüz) + 20 (yirmi) + 8 (sekiz) = **üç yüz yirmi sekiz**

**Example 3: 5,102**
*   5,000 (beş bin) + 100 (yüz) + 2 (iki) = **beş bin yüz iki**

**Example 4: 2,450,000**
*   2 million (iki milyon) + 400,000 (dört yüz bin) + 50,000 (elli bin) = **iki milyon dört yüz elli bin**
