Great — this is **İstanbul A1 Unit 5B**, and the main grammar is **“before / after”**. This unit is very important because Turkish uses **different structures depending on whether the word before/after is a noun or a verb**.

# 🇹🇷 Unit 5B — Önce / Sonra

## Before & After

The four structures you need to master are:

| Turkish                  | Meaning       |
| ------------------------ | ------------- |
| **isim + -DAn önce**     | before + noun |
| **isim + -DAn sonra**    | after + noun  |
| **fiil + -mAdAn önce**   | before + verb |
| **fiil + -DIktAn sonra** | after + verb  |

Let's build this slowly.

---

# 1. İsim + -DAn önce

### Before + noun

When the word after **before** is a **noun**, use:

> **NOUN + -DAn + önce**

Examples:

> **okuldan önce**
> before school

> **dersten önce**
> before the lesson

> **yemekten önce**
> before the meal

> **işten önce**
> before work

### Example

> **Arkadaşlarımla okuldan önce yemek yiyoruz.**

We eat with my friends **before school**.

Breakdown:

**okul + dan + önce**

school + from + before

---

# 2. İsim + -DAn sonra

### After + noun

Same structure:

> **NOUN + -DAn + sonra**

Examples:

> **okuldan sonra**
> after school

> **dersten sonra**
> after the lesson

> **işten sonra**
> after work

> **yemekten sonra**
> after the meal

Example:

> **Dersten sonra ne yapıyorsun?**

What do you do after class?

Answer:

> **Eve gidiyorum.**

I go home.

---

# 3. Why -DAn?

You already know **-DAn** from Turkish.

Normally:

> okuldan = from school

> evden = from home

> işten = from work

But when combined with **önce/sonra**, it gives the standard Turkish construction:

> okuldan önce = before school

> okuldan sonra = after school

So don't translate it word-for-word as "from school before."

Just memorize:

> **X-DAN ÖNCE = before X**

> **X-DAN SONRA = after X**

---

# 4. Verb + -mAdAn önce

### Before doing something

Now things become more interesting.

If you want to say:

> **before + VERB**

you use:

> **VERB + -mAdAn + önce**

Examples:

### Before sleeping

**uyu-** = sleep

> **uyumadan önce**

before sleeping

### Before going

**git-** = go

> **gitmeden önce**

before going

### Before coming

**gel-** = come

> **gelmeden önce**

before coming

### Before eating

**ye-** = eat

> **yemeden önce**

before eating

---

# 5. Examples

> **Uyumadan önce kitap okuyorum.**

I read a book before sleeping.

Breakdown:

**uyu + ma + dan + önce**

sleep + NEGATIVE/gerund form + from + before

Don't worry too much about the literal grammar yet. At A1, memorize the whole pattern:

> **-mAdAn önce = before doing**

---

Another example:

> **Toplantıya gelmeden önce hazırlık yapıyorum.**

I prepare before coming to the meeting.

**gelmeden önce**

= before coming

---

# 6. Very important: -mAdAn does NOT mean simply "not"

You might notice:

> gel**me**den önce

and think:

> gelme = don't come?

No.

Here:

> **gelmeden önce**

is a grammatical construction meaning:

> **before coming**

So:

> **Gitmeden önce yemek yiyorum.**

= I eat before going.

Not:

❌ "I eat before don't go."

---

# 7. Verb + -DIktAn sonra

### After doing something

For:

> **after + VERB**

use:

> **VERB + -DIktAn + sonra**

Examples:

**gitmek** → **gittikten sonra**

> after going

**gelmek** → **geldikten sonra**

> after coming

**yemek** → **yemekten sonra** / **yedikten sonra**

> after eating

**uyumak** → **uyuduktan sonra**

> after sleeping

---

# 8. Example from the book

> **Eve gittikten sonra ders çalışıyorum.**

I study after going home.

Breakdown:

**git + dik + ten + sonra**

go + after-form + from + after

The important thing:

> **gittikten sonra = after going**

---

Another example:

> **Sabah kalktıktan sonra duş alıyorum.**

I take a shower after getting up in the morning.

**kalkmak** → **kalktıktan sonra**

= after getting up

---

# 9. Compare all FOUR structures

This is the most important part of the lesson.

Let's use **school** and **go**.

### Noun

> **Okuldan önce**
> before school

### Noun

> **Okuldan sonra**
> after school

### Verb

> **Okula gitmeden önce**
> before going to school

### Verb

> **Okula gittikten sonra**
> after going to school

Notice:

**NOUN → -DAn**

**VERB → -mAdAn / -DIktAn**

# 11. The difference between noun and verb

This is exactly what your textbook is trying to teach.

### "Before school"

School = **noun**

> **okuldan önce**

### "Before going to school"

Going = **verb**

> **okula gitmeden önce**

---

### "After the lesson"

Lesson = **noun**

> **dersten sonra**

### "After studying"

Studying = **verb**

> **ders çalıştıktan sonra**

This distinction is crucial.

# 🧠 The A1 cheat sheet

Memorize these four patterns:

### ① NOUN + DAN + ÖNCE

**before + noun**

> okuldan önce
> before school

### ② NOUN + DAN + SONRA

**after + noun**

> okuldan sonra
> after school

### ③ VERB + MADAN + ÖNCE

**before + doing**

> gitmeden önce
> before going

### ④ VERB + DIKTAN + SONRA

**after + doing**

> gittikten sonra
> after going

---

## 🔥 The easiest way to remember

Think:

**NOUN**

> **-DAN önce** → before
> **-DAN sonra** → after

**VERB**

> **-MADAN önce** → before doing
> **-DIKTAN sonra** → after doing

### One sentence containing everything:

> **Okuldan önce kahvaltı yapıyorum. Okula gitmeden önce kahve içiyorum. Okuldan sonra eve gidiyorum. Eve gittikten sonra ders çalışıyorum.**

**Before school**, I have breakfast. **Before going to school**, I drink coffee. **After school**, I go home. **After going home**, I study.

If you can produce sentences like that naturally, you've understood the main grammar of **Unit 5B**.
