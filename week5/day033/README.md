# Turkish–English Study Guide: Ünite 1 — Yeni Bir Hayat

This unit is about moving to a new home, renting an apartment, finding a suitable neighborhood, and describing how you do things.

I'll explain the Turkish vocabulary and grammar in English, with examples you can use in daily conversations in Turkey.

### Understanding Turkish rental advertisements

1. Sahibinden kiralık

For rent directly from the owner.

Sahibinden means from the owner, usually without a real estate agent.

2. 2+1 daire

An apartment with two rooms/bedrooms plus one living room.

The same pattern applies to 1+1, 3+1, and so on.

3. Öğrenciye verilir

Available to students.

Öğrenci = student; -ye = to; verilir = is given/rented.

4. İki ay peşin ödemeli

Requires two months' payment in advance.

Peşin means in advance or upfront.

5. Pazarlık yok

No bargaining; the price is non-negotiable.

Important: the rental prices in your textbook are historical examples, not current Turkish housing prices.

## 2. The grammar — Zarf-fiiller

A zarf-fiil is a Turkish verb form that acts like an adverb. It can explain how an action happens, what happens repeatedly, or how actions relate to each other.

### A. Verb + -(y)ArAk — By doing something

This structure explains how an action is performed. It often translates as by doing, while doing, or doing something.

Formation: verb stem + -(y)arak / -(y)erek

The vowel changes according to Turkish vowel harmony.

| Verb                | Zarf-fiil  | English             |
| ------------------- | ---------- | ------------------- |
| yürü- (walk)        | yürüyerek  | by walking          |
| konuş- (speak)      | konuşarak  | by speaking         |
| dinle- (listen)     | dinleyerek | by listening        |
| oku- (read)         | okuyarak   | by reading          |
| koş- (run)          | koşarak    | by running          |
| çalış- (work/study) | çalışarak  | by working/studying |
| seyret- (watch)     | seyrederek | by watching         |

Examples:

* Her sabah okula yürüyerek gidiyorum. — I go to school on foot every morning.

* Müzik dinleyerek ders çalışıyorum. — I study while listening to music.

* Türkçeyi, Türk filmlerini seyrederek öğrendim. — I learned Turkish by watching Turkish films.

* Annesinin yanına koşarak gitti. — He/she ran to his/her mother.

### Special meanings: olmak and gitmek

Sometimes the same suffix creates a different meaning.

* Mühendis olarak çalışıyorum. — I work as an engineer.

* Hasta giderek iyileşiyor. — The patient is gradually getting better.

Here, olarak means as, while giderek means gradually / increasingly.

### Negative form: -(y)ArAk → -mayarak / -meyerek

This describes doing something without doing another action.

* Dersine çalışmayarak sınavdan kötü not aldı. — He/she got a bad grade by not studying.

* Benimle konuşmayarak kalbimi kırıyorsun. — You're breaking my heart by not talking to me.

Notice the negative verb stem:

* çalışmak → çalışmayarak

* konuşmak → konuşmayarak

* gitmek → gitmeyerek

### B. Verb + -(y)A + verb + -(y)A — Repeated actions

This structure emphasizes repetition or an action continuing over time. It often translates as by doing something again and again, after repeatedly doing something, or while doing something continuously.

| Turkish         | English                                 |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| konuşa konuşa   | by talking and talking                  |
| güle güle       | while laughing / with laughter          |
| güle oynaya     | joyfully, laughing and playing          |
| düşüne düşüne   | after thinking it over repeatedly       |
| anlata anlata   | by telling it again and again           |
| yapa yapa       | by doing it repeatedly                  |
| görmeye görmeye | from not seeing someone for a long time |
| çalışa çalışa   | by working/studying repeatedly          |

Examples from the lesson:

* Konuşa konuşa Türkçemi ilerlettim. — I improved my Turkish by talking a lot.

* Bilgisayarın karşısında çalışa çalışa gözlerim ağrıdı. — My eyes hurt from working at the computer for so long.

* Birbirimizi görmeye görmeye unuttuk. — We forgot each other because we hadn't seen each other for so long.

* Piknikte güle oynaya eğlendik. — We had fun at the picnic, laughing and playing.

Key difference:

* Türkçe öğrenerek — by learning Turkish.

* Türkçe konuşa konuşa — by speaking Turkish again and again, through practice.

The first focuses on the method; the second emphasizes repetition or the experience of doing it over time.

## 3. Reading comprehension — Nasıl bir ev arıyorsunuz?

What kind of home are you looking for?

Here is the conversation's essential information.

Gamze's situation

Gamze has started working in Istanbul and urgently needs a rental apartment near her workplace in Levent. She is single, does not know anyone in Istanbul yet, and will initially live alone.

Her requirements

* Location: Levent and nearby areas.

* Apartment: 1+1 or 2+1.

* Heating: natural gas.

* Security: a secure building.

* Budget: 1,000–1,500 TL in the textbook's historical context.

The real estate agent's two options

* Apartment A: 1,000 TL, larger in square meters.

* Apartment B: 1,250 TL, sea view, closer to the metro, tram, and bus.

Her decision

Gamze wants to see Apartment B because convenient access to public transportation is very important to her.

### Answers to exercise 5

1. Gamze Hanım nasıl bir ev arıyor?

Gamze Hanım, Levent'e yakın, kiralık, güvenlikli ve doğalgazlı bir ev arıyor. Bir oda bir salon ya da iki oda bir salon olabilir.

English: She is looking for a secure rental apartment with natural gas heating near Levent. It can be a 1+1 or 2+1.

2. Emlakçı hangi evleri öneriyor? Evler arasındaki farklar neler?

Emlakçı iki ev öneriyor. Birinci ev 1.000 lira ve daha büyük. İkinci ev 1.250 lira, deniz manzaralı ve ulaşım araçlarına daha yakın.

English: The first apartment is cheaper and larger. The second is more expensive, has a sea view, and is closer to public transportation.

3. Gamze Hanım hangi evi görmek istiyor? Neden?

Gamze Hanım ikinci evi görmek istiyor çünkü ulaşım araçlarına yakın olması onun için çok önemli.

English: She wants to see the second apartment because being close to public transportation is very important to her.

4. Sizce Gamze Hanım evi beğenecek mi?

Bence Gamze Hanım evi beğenecek çünkü ev deniz manzaralı ve ulaşım araçlarına yakın.

English: I think Gamze will like the apartment because it has a sea view and is close to public transportation.

## 4. Useful speaking phrases

These phrases will help you discuss moving to a new city or finding accommodation in Turkey.

| Turkish                           | English                                   |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Nasıl bir ev arıyorsunuz?         | What kind of home are you looking for?    |
| Kiralık bir ev arıyorum.          | I'm looking for a place to rent.          |
| İş yerime yakın olsun istiyorum.  | I want it to be close to my workplace.    |
| Tek başıma yaşayacağım.           | I will live alone.                        |
| Ev eşyalı mı?                     | Is the apartment furnished?               |
| Aidat dâhil mi?                   | Is the building maintenance fee included? |
| Depozito ne kadar?                | How much is the security deposit?         |
| Kira sözleşmesi yapabilir miyiz?  | Can we make a rental agreement?           |
| Toplu taşımaya yakın mı?          | Is it close to public transportation?     |
| Fiyatta pazarlık yapabilir miyiz? | Can we negotiate the price?               |
| Evi görmek istiyorum.             | I want to see the apartment.              |
| Bu ev bütçeme uygun.              | This apartment fits my budget.            |

A useful sentence for your own accommodation search:

Üniversiteme yakın, uygun fiyatlı ve eşyalı bir oda arıyorum.

I'm looking for an affordable, furnished room near my university.

### Exercise 4 — Complete the sentences

Use the vocabulary list: kira, taşınmak, nezih, merkezî sistem, kira sözleşmesi, depozito, metrekare, muhit, manzara, kombi.
