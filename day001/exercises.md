## 🧠 Exercise 1: Vowel Harmony (The Golden Rule)
**The Rule:** 
*   **Back Vowels** (a, ı, o, u) take **Back Suffixes** (-lar, -da).
*   **Front Vowels** (e, i, ö, ü) take **Front Suffixes** (-ler, -de).

## 📍 Exercise 2: Grammatical Cases (To, At, From)
**The Rule:**
*   **Dative (-a / -e):** To / Toward
*   **Locative (-da / -de):** At / In / On
*   **Ablative (-dan / -den):** From / Out of

**Task:** Fill in the blank with the correct case suffix for the noun in parentheses. Don't forget vowel harmony!

1. Ben **(İstanbul)** _______ yaşıyorum. *(I live **in** Istanbul.)*
2. Biz **(market)** _______ gidiyoruz. *(We are going **to** the market.)*
3. Ben **(ev)** _______ geldim. *(I came **from** home.)*
4. O **(okul)** _______ çalışıyor. *(He/She is studying **at** school.)*
5. Sen **(iş)** _______ geliyor musun? *(Are you coming **from** work?)*

## 🎒 Exercise 3: "Var" and "Yok" (Possession)
**The Rule:** Turkish doesn't use a verb for "to have". It uses the formula: **[Noun] + [Possessive Suffix] + var / yok**.
*   **-(ı)m** = my
*   **-(ı)n** = your

**Vocabulary Bank:** 
*   *Para* (Money)
*   *Zaman* (Time)
*   *Araba* (Car)
*   *Sorun* (Problem)

## 🚫 Exercise 4: "İstemek" (To Want vs. Not Want)
**The Rule:** The negative suffix **-me/-ma** is inserted *before* the tense suffix.
*   *iste-* (want) + *-iyor-* (present) + *-um* (I) = **istiyorum**
*   *iste-* (want) + **-me-** (not) + *-iyor-* (present) + *-um* (I) = **istemiyorum**

**Task:** Translate these sentences using the patterns you learned.

4. I don't want to sleep. *(uyumak)* ➔ ____________________
