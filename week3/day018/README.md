# ⭐ GRAMMAR — GEÇMİŞ ZAMAN

## PAST TENSE

This page introduces the Turkish **-DI past tense**, used for completed actions in the past.

### Positive

**gitmek → gittim**
**to go → I went**

**okumak → okudum**
**to read → I read**

**seyretmek → seyrettim**
**to watch → I watched**

**çalışmak → çalıştım**
**to study → I studied**

**icat etmek → icat etti**
**to invent → he/she invented**

---

## Negative

The negative is made with **-ma / -me** before the past-tense ending.

**çalıştım** → I studied

**çalışmadım** → I didn't study

Example:

**Hayır, ders çalışmadım.**
**No, I didn't study.**

---

## Questions

The question particle is:

**mı / mi / mu / mü**

Examples:

**Ders çalıştın mı?**
**Did you study?**

**Sinemaya gittin mi?**
**Did you go to the cinema?**

**Kitabı okudun mu?**
**Did you read the book?**

# Belirli Geçmiş Zaman (-DI)

## Simple Past Tense

**Bu kip, yüklemin anlattığı işin, oluşun ya da hareketin, sözün söylendiği zamandan önce yapıldığını ve bitirildiğini anlatır.**

**This tense indicates that an action, event, or situation happened and was completed before the time of speaking.**

---

# -DI PAST TENSE

| Person            | Olumlu — Positive          | Olumsuz — Negative                  | Olumlu Soru — Positive Question     | Olumsuz Soru — Negative Question         |
| ----------------- | -------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| **Ben** — I       | **geldim** — I came        | **gelmedim** — I didn't come        | **geldim mi?** — Did I come?        | **gelmedim mi?** — Didn't I come?        |
| **Sen** — You     | **geldin** — you came      | **gelmedin** — you didn't come      | **geldin mi?** — Did you come?      | **gelmedin mi?** — Didn't you come?      |
| **O** — He/She/It | **geldi** — he/she came    | **gelmedi** — he/she didn't come    | **geldi mi?** — Did he/she come?    | **gelmedi mi?** — Didn't he/she come?    |
| **Biz** — We      | **geldik** — we came       | **gelmedik** — we didn't come       | **geldik mi?** — Did we come?       | **gelmedik mi?** — Didn't we come?       |
| **Siz** — You     | **geldiniz** — you came    | **gelmediniz** — you didn't come    | **geldiniz mi?** — Did you come?    | **gelmediniz mi?** — Didn't you come?    |
| **Onlar** — They  | **geldi(ler)** — they came | **gelmedi(ler)** — they didn't come | **geldi(ler) mi?** — Did they come? | **gelmedi(ler) mi?** — Didn't they come? |

> **Note:** With **onlar (they)**, the **-ler/-lar** ending can sometimes be omitted when the subject is already clear.

# ⭐ THE -DI PATTERN

### Positive

**gel + di + m → geldim**
**gel + di + n → geldin**
**gel + di → geldi**
**gel + di + k → geldik**
**gel + di + niz → geldiniz**
**gel + di + ler → geldiler**

### Negative

Add **-ma / -me** before the past ending:

**gelmedim** = I didn't come
**gelmedin** = you didn't come
**gelmedi** = he/she didn't come
**gelmedik** = we didn't come
**gelmediniz** = you didn't come
**gelmediler** = they didn't come

### Question

The question particle **mı / mi / mu / mü** comes **after the conjugated verb**:

**Geldin mi?**
→ Did you come?

**Geldiniz mi?**
→ Did you come?

**Gelmedin mi?**
→ Didn't you come?

**Gelmediniz mi?**
→ Didn't you come?

---

# 🧠 A2 RULE OF THUMB

**-DI = a completed action in the past.**

**Yesterday → dün**
**Last week → geçen hafta**
**Last year → geçen yıl / geçen sene**
**Two years ago → iki yıl önce**
**A little while ago → biraz önce**

Examples:

**Dün geldim.** → I came yesterday.
**Geçen hafta gittim.** → I went last week.
**İki yıl önce mezun oldum.** → I graduated two years ago.
**Biraz önce yemek yedim.** → I ate a little while ago.

### Remember:

**Positive:**
**gel-d-i-m → geldim**

**Negative:**
**gel-me-d-i-m → gelmedim**

**Question:**
**gel-d-i-n mi? → geldin mi?**

**Negative question:**
**gel-me-d-i-n mi? → gelmedin mi?**

# ⭐ MAIN A2 PATTERN

This entire exercise is practicing:

### **Subject + Verb(-DI) + Other Information**

**Ben kitap okudum.**
I read a book.

**Fatih Sultan Mehmet İstanbul'u fethetti.**
Fatih Sultan Mehmet conquered Istanbul.

**Galileo uzay araştırmaları yaptı.**
Galileo conducted space research.

**Orhan Pamuk romanlar yazdı.**
Orhan Pamuk wrote novels.

### Negative

**Ders çalışmadım.**
I didn't study.

**Film seyretmedim.**
I didn't watch a movie.

### Question

**Ne yaptın?**
What did you do?

**Hafta sonu ne yaptınız?**
What did you do at the weekend?

**Daha önce seyrettiniz mi?**
Did you watch it before?

# 🎯 SPEAKING PRACTICE

A very useful A2 answer to **"Hafta sonu ne yaptın?"** is:

**Hafta sonu ders çalıştım, kitap okudum, müzik dinledim ve arkadaşlarımla buluştum. Pazar günü sahilde yürüdüm.**

**At the weekend, I studied, read a book, listened to music, and met up with my friends. On Sunday, I walked on the beach.**

This lets you practice several **-DI past-tense verbs in one natural answer**.
