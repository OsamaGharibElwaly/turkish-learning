### The important insight

Don't study Turkish vocabulary as:

> **gelmek = to come**

Study it as a **family**:

**gelmek** → to come
**geliyorum** → I am coming
**geldim** → I came
**geleceğim** → I will come
**gelmek istiyorum** → I want to come
**gelebilir miyim?** → Can I come?
**gelmem lazım** → I need to come
**gelme!** → Don't come!
**gelelim** → Let's come/go and come

This is especially powerful in Turkish because **one root can generate a huge number of conversational forms**.

If you're currently around **A1 Turkish**, I'd prioritize **verbs + question words + connectors + everyday chunks** over memorizing hundreds of nouns. That will give you much more conversational ability per hour.
