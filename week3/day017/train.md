## 🧠 Your 30-second rule

Remember the **meaning**, not just the suffix:

### **-LI = HAS / WITH**

* **sakallı** = with a beard / bearded
* **saçlı** = with hair
* **şekerli** = with sugar
* **tuzlu** = salty / with salt
* **dikkatli** = careful

### **-SIZ = WITHOUT / LACKING**

* **sakalsız** = without a beard
* **şekersiz** = without sugar
* **tuzsuz** = without salt
* **dikkatsiz** = careless
* **zararsız** = harmless

### 🔥 And remember vowel harmony:

**-lı / -li / -lu / -lü**
**-sız / -siz / -suz / -süz**

Don't worry about memorizing which one individually—**look at the last vowel of the noun**.
