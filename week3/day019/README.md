# 2. İsim Cümlelerinde Belirli Geçmiş Zaman

This is the most important part of the page.

In English:

> I **was** sick.

In Turkish, we attach the past-tense ending to the adjective/noun.

The question particle **mı/mi/mu/mü** comes before the past ending.

> hasta **mıydım?**

> hasta **mıydın?**
> Were you sick?

> Was he/she sick?

# 4. The pattern you should memorize

Think of it like this:

### Positive

**adjective/noun + y + past-person ending**

> hasta + y + dım → **hastaydım**

### Negative

**adjective/noun + değil + past-person ending**

> hasta + değil + dim → **hasta değildim**

### Question

**adjective/noun + mı + y + past-person ending**

> hasta + mı + ydım → **hasta mıydım?**

The **y** is used as a buffer because the sounds need to connect naturally.

# Bağlaçlar — Connectors

**Bağlaç** = conjunction / connector

These words connect two sentences and show a **cause → result** relationship.

| Turkish        | English                      | Position                      |
| -------------- | ---------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| **çünkü**      | because                      | usually introduces the reason |
| **bu sebeple** | for this reason / therefore  | introduces the result         |
| **bu nedenle** | for this reason / therefore  | introduces the result         |
| **bu yüzden**  | therefore / that's why       | introduces the result         |
| **bunun için** | for this reason / that's why | introduces the result         |

---

## 1. Çünkü = Because

> Geçen hafta okula gitmedim. **Çünkü hastaydım.**

I didn't go to school last week. **Because I was sick.**

Structure:

**Result → Çünkü → Reason**

> Okula gitmedim → çünkü hastaydım.

---

## 2. Bu sebeple = For this reason

> Hastaydım. **Bu sebeple** geçen hafta okula gitmedim.

I was sick. **For this reason**, I didn't go to school last week.

Structure:

**Reason → Bu sebeple → Result**

---

## 3. Bu nedenle = For this reason / Therefore

> Hastaydım. **Bu nedenle** okula gitmedim.

I was sick. **Therefore**, I didn't go to school.

---

## 4. Bu yüzden = Therefore / That's why

> Kitabı çok beğendim. **Bu yüzden** size de tavsiye ediyorum.

I really liked the book. **That's why** I recommend it to you too.

---

## 5. Bunun için = For this reason / That's why

> Dün çok meşguldüm. **Bunun için** seni aramadım.

I was very busy yesterday. **That's why** I didn't call you.

> Geçen yıl tatile Paris’e gittik ve çok para harcadık. **Bu nedenle** bu yıl tatile gitmiyoruz.

Last year we went to Paris for vacation and spent a lot of money. **Therefore**, we're not going on vacation this year.

### 4.

> Aylinler geçen hafta bize gelmediler. **Çünkü** onlar Japonya’ya gitti.

Aylin and her family didn't come to us last week. **Because** they went to Japan.

### 5.

> Sınavdan çok az puan aldım. **Bu yüzden** annem bana kızdı.

I got a very low score on the exam. **That's why** my mother got angry with me.

### Answers only:

**1. çünkü**
**2. bu yüzden**
**3. bu nedenle**
**4. çünkü**
**5. bu yüzden**

# 6. Write 5 sentences

Here are simple **A2-level examples** using each connector:

1. **Dün çok hastaydım çünkü hava çok soğuktu.**
   I was very sick yesterday because the weather was very cold.

2. **Çok çalıştım. Bu sebeple sınavdan iyi puan aldım.**
   I studied a lot. For this reason, I got a good score on the exam.

3. **Geç kaldım. Bu nedenle otobüsü kaçırdım.**
   I was late. Therefore, I missed the bus.

4. **Hava çok sıcaktı. Bu yüzden dışarı çıkmadım.**
   The weather was very hot. That's why I didn't go outside.

5. **Yarın sınavım var. Bunun için bugün ders çalışacağım.**
   I have an exam tomorrow. For this reason, I will study today.

## 🧠 The easiest way to remember them

Think of them as two groups:

### **REASON → RESULT**

**Because:**

> **çünkü**

**Result:**

> **bu sebeple**
> **bu nedenle**
> **bu yüzden**
> **bunun için**

So:

> **Hastaydım. → Bu yüzden → okula gitmedim.**

**I was sick → That's why → I didn't go to school.**

But you can reverse it with **çünkü**:

> **Okula gitmedim → çünkü → hastaydım.**

**I didn't go to school → because → I was sick.**

That's the key distinction this page wants you to learn.
